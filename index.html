🔍 Scout - Market Intelligence Platform

Overview: Scout is an AI-powered competitive intelligence platform that automates market monitoring for product teams and strategy leaders. It scrapes competitor sources, uses LLM-based classification to extract strategic events, and delivers actionable intelligence briefings.

Description: Scout transforms hours of manual competitor research into 60-second insights. The platform monitors SaaS competitors across Analytics, Design Tools, and Project Management verticals, automatically detecting feature launches, pricing changes, and strategic partnerships. Key features include:

  • Automated Data Collection: Multi-source scraping from blogs, changelogs, and RSS feeds
  • AI-Powered Classification: GPT-4-mini with structured outputs for event extraction
  • Interactive Dashboard: Real-time metrics, category breakdowns, and confidence scoring
  • Intelligence Briefings: One-click HTML export with visualizations and source links
  • Competitive Sets: Pre-configured monitoring for Mixpanel, Figma, Asana, and more

Impact: Reduces competitive intelligence gathering from 3+ hours per week to under 60 seconds, achieving 80%+ classification precision on real-world competitor announcements.

Technologies Used: Python, Streamlit, OpenAI GPT-4-mini, SQLite, Plotly, Newspaper3k, Feedparser
