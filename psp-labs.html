Your dashboards aren't failing you.
Your reporting system is.

PSP Labs designs and implements structured reporting systems for operations-driven businesses — so executives can trust their numbers and make decisions with confidence. Fixed scope. Two phases. No surprises.

The Problem

You have data. You don't trust it.

The same KPI shows different values in different reports. Every leadership meeting starts with someone reconciling numbers before anything else gets discussed. Decisions slow down — or worse, they get made on the wrong data.

Root cause The issue isn't your dashboards. It's the absence of a reporting architecture underneath them. Without a structured data model and defined business logic, every report is an island — and every number is a question.
Who This Is For

Best-fit clients

  • Operations-driven businesses (retail, supply chain, manufacturing, logistics)
  • 20–250 employees
  • COO or Ops Lead owns reporting
  • No dedicated internal analytics team
  • Already using some tools — ERP, Excel, POS, or Power BI

Presenting symptoms

  • Conflicting numbers across reports
  • Manual reconciliation before every meeting
  • Low trust in dashboards
  • Business logic scattered across spreadsheets
  • Slow or hesitant decision-making

A reporting system, built right. Once.
